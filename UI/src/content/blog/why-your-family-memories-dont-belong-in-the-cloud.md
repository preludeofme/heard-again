Aeterna's Time Capsule claims to offer an "entirely offline… no subscription, no dependency on any platform, server, or company." It promises a physical hardware device that stores your complete digital archive, your voice, your stories, your digital twin, all without internet.

Sounds appealing at first glance. We get it—nobody's looking to hand over control of their family's voice to another company. But this is just another name for a lock-in strategy in the wrong package.

## The Reality of the "Self-Hosted" Market

Aeterna's approach puts you in a box—literally. Their hardware solution requires you to trust them (via the physical hardware) and doesn't give you full control of your family's data. It's a closed system where:

- You pay $1,499 upfront for physical hardware
- Your data stays in cloud databases they control
- The "offline" benefits are based on their hardware being physically in your home
- Future upgrades, security patches, and features come from them

Aeterna isn't giving you back your voice, they're giving you a physical box that requires ongoing support from a company that may very well no longer exist.

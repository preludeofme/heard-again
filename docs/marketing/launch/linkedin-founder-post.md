# Heard Again — LinkedIn Founder Post

**Format:** Personal story post, 3-4 short paragraphs, ends with gentle CTA

---

A year ago, I realized something that's been quietly bothering me ever since.

I have thousands of photos of my family. But I can't remember what my grandmother sounded like.

Not her laugh. Not the way she said my name. Not the stories she told at every holiday dinner. Fourteen years, and the sound is just... gone.

That realization turned into a project, and that project turned into something much bigger than I expected.

I built **Heard Again** — an open-source platform for preserving family voices and stories. Record conversations. Upload old audio. Build voice profiles. Create a living archive of the people you love, owned entirely by your family.

It's MIT-licensed, self-hostable, and built from the ground up with one principle: **consent first, always.** Every recording has a verified consent trail. Every family controls their own data. No company servers, no data mining, no "we might use this to train models."

This is the kind of technology I want to exist in the world. Tools that help families preserve what matters, without extracting value from their most personal moments.

If this resonates — check it out at heardagain.com. Star it on GitHub if you want to follow along.

And if there's one thing I hope you take away: go record a conversation with someone you love this week. The technology will be ready when you are. But the voice won't wait.

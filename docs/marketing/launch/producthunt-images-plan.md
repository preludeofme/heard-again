# ProductHunt Gallery Images Plan

**Reference OG image:** https://heardagain.com/og-image.png
**Gallery spec:** 5 images, 1270×760px each
**Brand palette:** Navy #16334a, cream #fcf9f4
**Tone:** Warm, editorial, human-first — like a quality magazine spread, not a SaaS dashboard

---

## Image 1: Hero / Brand Introduction
**What to show:** The Heard Again wordmark centered on a warm cream background (#fcf9f4), with the tagline "Family stories, preserved with care." set in a refined serif below it. A subtle, soft-focus background element — perhaps a vintage family photograph faded to near-transparency — sits behind the text.

**Suggested caption:** "Heard Again — an open-source platform for preserving the voices and stories that matter most."

**Design notes:** This should match or complement the existing OG image at heardagain.com/og-image.png. Use the same navy-and-cream palette. Keep it restrained and magazine-like.

---

## Image 2: The Emotional Hook
**What to show:** A warm, editorial-style photograph of an older person (grandparent) speaking to a child, seen from slightly behind — we see the child listening intently, the grandparent's face partially visible, animated in conversation. The image should feel candid, not staged. No screenshots. No UI.

**Suggested caption:** "Record the stories you'll want to hear again."

**Design notes:** Use Unsplash or similar for a high-quality, emotionally resonant stock photo. Crop to 1270×760. Warm tones, natural light. No overlays except possibly a subtle navy-tinted gradient at the bottom for caption placement.

---

## Image 3: Voice Lab Feature
**What to show:** A clean, minimal screenshot of the Voice Lab interface — showing a voice profile card with a waveform visualization and the text "Mom's Voice — Captured March 2025." Below it, a simple indicator: "22 family recordings • 3 voice samples preserved." The UI should feel calm and archival, not technical. Navy accents on cream background.

**Suggested caption:** "Voice Lab captures what matters — not to replace anyone, but to preserve the sound of who they are."

**Design notes:** Frame this as a magazine-style feature image. The screenshot should occupy roughly 60% of the image, with the rest being clean cream whitespace and a small caption. Avoid looking like a typical SaaS product screenshot.

---

## Image 4: Open Source & Family Ownership
**What to show:** A split composition. Left side: a warm family photograph or illustration (multi-generational). Right side: a dark navy panel (#16334a) with white text reading "Open source. Self-hostable. Your family's data stays yours. Always." with the GitHub mark subtly placed below. The split should feel like a magazine layout — editorial, not marketing.

**Suggested caption:** "Fully open source under the MIT license. Inspect the code, run it on your own server, or use our cloud plans starting at $4.99/month."

**Design notes:** This is the "trust" image. It should communicate transparency and data sovereignty. The navy panel should feel solid and reassuring, not cold. GitHub: https://github.com/preludeofme/heard-again

---

## Image 5: What's Next / Call to Action
**What to show:** A warm cream background with a centered, editorial headline: "Start your family archive this week." Below it, smaller text: "Open source. Consent-first. Free to self-host." and a subtle prompt: "→ heardagain.com / github.com/preludeofme/heard-again". The composition should feel inviting, like the closing spread of a feature article.

**Suggested caption:** "Record someone you love this week. Heard Again is free, open source, and ready when you are."

**Design notes:** This is the closing image — warm, encouraging, not pushy. Use the same navy-on-cream palette as Image 1 for visual bookending. No buttons, no urgency, no "Sign up now" — just an invitation.

# ProductHunt Launch Day Checklist

## 2 Weeks Before Launch

- [ ] **Finalize tagline and description** — lock in the primary options from `producthunt-tagline.txt` and `producthunt-description.txt`
- [ ] **Prepare all 5 gallery images** — design per `producthunt-images-plan.md`, export at 1270×760px
- [ ] **Create or claim ProductHunt maker profile** — ensure Ryan Buck's profile is complete with photo, bio, Twitter/GitHub links
- [ ] **Join relevant ProductHunt communities** — engage in "Open Source," "Family," and "Artificial Intelligence" topic discussions (not promoting yet, just participating genuinely)
- [ ] **Line up 3–5 friends/early users** who are willing to leave thoughtful, specific comments on launch day (ProductHunt's algorithm weights early engagement heavily)
- [ ] **Prep the maker comment** — review `producthunt-maker-comment.md`, personalize further if needed, have it ready to paste immediately after scheduling
- [ ] **Test the website** — verify heardagain.com loads fast, signup works, OG image displays correctly in social previews (use metatags.io or similar)
- [ ] **Prepare social posts** — draft 2–3 tweets/LinkedIn posts announcing the launch, ready to publish on launch day

## 3 Days Before Launch

- [ ] **Schedule the ProductHunt listing** — ProductHunt allows scheduling up to 3 days ahead. Schedule for Tuesday/Wednesday/Thursday (these days perform best). Set launch time to 12:01 AM PT (midnight Pacific) to maximize the full voting day.
- [ ] **Fill in all listing fields:**
  - Tagline (60 chars max)
  - Description (260 chars max)
  - Topics (3–5 from `producthunt-topics.txt`)
  - Gallery images (5 images)
  - Maker comment (paste from `producthunt-maker-comment.md`)
  - Website: https://heardagain.com
  - GitHub: https://github.com/preludeofme/heard-again
  - Pricing: Free (self-hosted), $4.99–$39.99/month (cloud)
- [ ] **Verify all links work** — website, GitHub, social links in maker profile
- [ ] **Notify early supporters** — send a quick message to the 3–5 people you lined up, reminding them launch is coming and when to check in
- [ ] **Prep the "first hour"** — set an alarm for 12:01 AM PT. Be online, monitor comments, reply to every single one within 30 minutes. PH's algorithm heavily rewards maker responsiveness.

## Launch Day

### At Launch (12:01 AM PT)
- [ ] **Confirm the listing is live** — open ProductHunt and verify everything renders correctly
- [ ] **Post the maker comment immediately** if not pre-filled during scheduling
- [ ] **Reply to early comments within 15–30 minutes** — be warm, genuine, not salesy
- [ ] **Share on personal social channels:**
  - Twitter/X: "After months of building, Heard Again is live on @ProductHunt. An open-source platform for preserving family voices — consent-first, family-owned, free to self-host. Would mean the world if you checked it out. 🧵👇 [link]"
  - LinkedIn: Longer post about why you built it (adapt from maker comment)
  - Relevant Discord/Slack communities (if appropriate and allowed)

### Throughout the Day
- [ ] **Reply to every comment** — even a simple "thank you" goes a long way. Prioritize thoughtful replies to substantive questions.
- [ ] **Do NOT ask for upvotes** — against PH rules and hurts credibility. Let the product speak.
- [ ] **Monitor the leaderboard** — Products that hit top 5 by mid-morning PT tend to stay there. If you're close, share again mid-day.
- [ ] **Engage with other launches** — genuine, supportive comments on other products launching that day build goodwill (and sometimes reciprocal attention)
- [ ] **Track referral sources** — check heardagain.com analytics to see where traffic is coming from
- [ ] **Thank supporters publicly** — a tweet or post thanking early commenters and upvoters goes a long way

### Day After
- [ ] **Send a thank-you email** to everyone who signed up from ProductHunt traffic (if you have email capture)
- [ ] **Write a post-launch reflection** — tweet or blog about what you learned, what surprised you
- [ ] **Follow up on feature requests** — any feature ideas that came from PH comments? Log them in GitHub issues and reply to the commenter letting them know
- [ ] **Archive results** — screenshot your final ranking, vote count, comment count, and traffic stats for your records
- [ ] **Update GitHub README** — add a "Featured on ProductHunt" badge/link if appropriate

## Quick Reference: What NOT to Do

- ❌ **Do NOT** ask for upvotes — even casually ("if you like it, an upvote would help…")
- ❌ **Do NOT** use banned words: "resurrect," "reanimate," "digital immortality," "bring back"
- ❌ **Do NOT** lead with AI/tech language in replies — keep it human, warm, editorial
- ❌ **Do NOT** spam other launches with "check out mine too" comments
- ❌ **Do NOT** create fake accounts to upvote — PH detects this and will penalize or remove the listing
- ❌ **Do NOT** ignore comments — every unanswered comment is a missed relationship

# Facebook Group Post — Family History / Genealogy Groups

**Tone:** community-oriented, question-first, invite stories. No hard sell.

---

Quick question for the group, and I'd genuinely love stories in the comments:

Do you have a recording of your grandparents' or parents' voices? Not a video of a birthday — just their voice talking, telling a story, the way they actually sounded?

I was looking through my photos the other day and realized something. I have hundreds of pictures of my grandfather, and almost no recording of his voice. A photo freezes a face, but a recording keeps the sound of someone — the way they laughed, the pauses before they said something that mattered.

I sat with him once and asked how he met my grandmother. The story went forty minutes in six directions and I meant to record it and never did. The words are blurring now.

If you've been meaning to do this, here's the one tip that made the biggest difference for me: don't call it an "interview." Just name one specific memory. "Could you tell me how you and grandpa met? Mind if I record it so I don't forget the details?" Most people relax once they forget the recorder is there — and that's when the best stories show up.

And a question that never misses: "What did your childhood home sound like in the morning?" Sensory questions get past the yes/no answers.

So I'd love to hear from you all: if you've recorded a family member, what one question got them to really open up? And if you never did, what do you wish you'd asked while you still could? ❤️

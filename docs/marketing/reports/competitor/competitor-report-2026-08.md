## Executive Summary (Top 3 Insights)

1. **MyHeritage's "voice" is recorded-human, not AI-cloned — the whitespace Heard Again owns is still empty.** DeepStory (their only AI-voice product) was **discontinued in August 2025**; its successor LiveMemory™ is photo *animation*, not voice. Their closest move — MyStories **audio QR codes (Aug 13, 2026)** — replays a loved one's *own human recording*, and their AI Editor / Scribe AI operate on *text*, not voice synthesis. **No major competitor today clones a real family member's voice.** Confirmed directly from MyHeritage's Knowledge Base.

2. **StoryCorps and FamilySearch are doubling down away from AI voice, not toward it.** StoryCorps launched **Connect250** (a human-first "time capsule" with NPR, June 2026) and a physical **Prego** recording-device partnership (April 2026); their tech leadership explicitly frames AI as a "get out of the way" concern, and their only AI surface is a *gated intake form screening external AI research requests*. FamilySearch's 2026 AI roadmap is entirely text/search (handwriting OCR, chatbot, tree hints) — zero voice. The "AI-native StoryCorps" category slot remains vacant.

3. **StoryFile — the previously dormant AI-conversational-video company — is back and institutionalized.** It emerged from Chapter 11 (Mar 2025), was acquired (Mar 2025), and now operates as **"Authentic Interactions"**, making a CNN debut (May 2026), launching a "Digital Likeness Directive," and building Kara Swisher's digital twin. Its pivot is toward museums/institutions and conversational *video* of public figures — not self-hosted family voice preservation. The closest grief-tech entrants (YOV/"Versona", Solace) are proprietary chatbots/apps, not self-hosted + open-source voice cloning.

## 1. MyHeritage — ⚠️ HIGH THREAT (converging, but NOT on voice cloning)

**New product features (material):**

- **MyStories update (Aug 13, 2026)** — the memory keepsake-book product. Confirmed via official blog:
  - **Audio QR codes** — printed books embed QR codes that play the storyteller's *own recorded voice* in any phone browser (no app/login). **Note:** plays the person's *human* recording, not an AI-generated voice.
  - **AI Editor** — two modes ("Fix errors" and "Adjust style"); edits text, does *not* generate content.
  - **Dedication page**, **side-by-side photos (up to 3)**, **photo rotation**.
  - URL: https://blog.myheritage.com/2026/08/new-in-mystories-ai-editor-audio-story-playback-and-more/
- **Y-DNA (paternal lineage)** — Aug 17, 2026.
- **Scribe AI** (document transcription/translation/interpretation) — Mar 5, 2026; mobile Apr 23, 2026. Transcribes *written* records, not voice.
- **Family Infographics** — May 7, 2026 (life story → visual artwork).
- **Full-genome DNA** — announced at RootsTech 2026.

**DeepStory (their only AI-voice product) — DISCONTINUED Aug 2025,** confirmed in MyHeritage's own Knowledge Base: *"The DeepStory feature has been discontinued to make way for new and exciting innovations… try LiveMemory™."* DeepStory was generic TTS narration (D-ID video reenactment), never true voice cloning.

**Strategic direction (RootsTech 2026, March):** 2026 AI investment concentrated on genealogical research (Scribe AI, Gaia in-account research assistant) and photo animation (LiveMemory, Family Infographics) — deliberately "anchored to real source material so it doesn't hallucinate." Voice is peripheral.

**Keywords targeted:** From official homepage `<meta name="keywords">` — genealogy, family tree, historical records, DNA, pedigree, surnames, census. **No voice/voice-cloning/audio-memory/storytelling keywords at all.** (Interpretation: they are not targeting voice-preservation SEO.)

**Threat assessment:** Direct in the *neighboring* memory keep-sake space, but **the specific "clone a real loved one's voice + self-host" position is unoccupied by MyHeritage.** Differentiation must lean on consent, data ownership, open source, and true voice cloning of a living family member — not "we do memory books too."

## 2. StoryCorps — LOW threat (human-first, expanding)

**Activity:**
- **Connect250** (flagship 2026) — nationwide conversation project for America's 250th; NPR *Morning Edition* partnership "time capsule of America's voices." NPR announced **Jun 17, 2026**; StoryCorps pages Jun 16 / Jun 1, 2026.
- **Prego partnership (Apr 2026)** — a *physical hardware* dinner-table recording device branded to the archive ("$20 to get your voice in the Library of Congress"). Covered by WIRED, The Verge, Fast Company. A recorder, not voice cloning.
- **"Stories to Start the School Year"** (Jul 30, 2026); **Brightness in Black** continuing (Marshawn Lynch ep, Jul 23, 2026).

**Product / AI posture:**
- **No AI voice, no voice cloning.** "The StoryCorps App Turns 11" (Mar 17, 2026) — Director of Digital & Tech Innovation: technology *"in service of something deeply human… protect the irreplaceable… recording of a never-again moment."* Forward plans = surfacing library, curated prompts, story-matching. Zero synthetic voice.
- **"AI Research Proposal" page (May 19, 2026)** — a *gated intake/screening form* for external AI/data researchers demanding ethics/safety/privacy alignment. This is a governance gate to *screen* AI requests, not a product roadmap.

**Keywords:** Connect250, time capsule, conversation, archive, Library of Congress, One Small Step, record your story. **Absent:** voice cloning, synthetic, AI voice.

**Threat assessment:** Not a commercial threat; arguably further from Heard Again's space than ever. Its philosophical opposite-of-AI-voice posture is a positioning opportunity: Heard Again can be framed as "the AI-native, self-owned alternative to StoryCorps" without StoryCorps competing at all.

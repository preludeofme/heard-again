## 3. Broken Links & Redirects

### Critical Finding: 307 Temporary Redirects
| URL Pattern | Redirect | Type | Issue |
|-------------|----------|------|-------|
| `heardagain.com` → `www.heardagain.com` | All pages | **307** (Temporary) | Should be **301** (Permanent) for SEO |

All `heardagain.com` URLs redirect to `www.heardagain.com` with a **307 Temporary Redirect**. Search engines treat 307 as a temporary move — they index the non-www domain rather than consolidating to `www`. This dilutes page rank across two domain variants.

**Recommendation:** Change the redirect to **301 Permanent** in the Vercel/Cloudflare configuration.
